## Career

I started as an engineer and founder in Seoul's early mobile and commerce scene, then led creative technology there. After that I built and scaled engineering teams from Singapore: travel, telemedicine, and logistics. I went back to founding and operating, including a seed round, an interim CEO seat, digital leadership in Dubai, and a residency in Riyadh. Today I build AI myself and still lead delivery.

### Applied AI, 2025 to now

**AI consultant and lead AI solution architect, Jul 2026 to present.** Independent contractor at an AI platform company.

I put work systems and processes in place within 60 days and led its move toward a platform business. I advise and mentor the founders. The products I architected are [LLM Gateway](https://gateway.chatforgoodtech.com/), [Agent Orchestrator](https://orchestrator.chatforgoodtech.com/), Chat4Physio, and Guardian Watch.

**Entrepreneur in Residence, [Antler](https://www.antler.co/), Oct 2025 to Dec 2025, Riyadh.** I cofounded FMInsight during the residency, an AI agent platform for facility management in Grade A commercial buildings across the GCC. I defined the product strategy, the MVP architecture, and the go to market plan for building owners and operators.

**AI instructor, Jun 2026 to present.** See [Mentoring and teaching](#mentoring-and-teaching).

### Founder and operator, 2022 to 2025

**Digital Director, [MBLM](https://mblm.com/), Nov 2024 to Sep 2025, Dubai.** I led applied AI commercialization and digital product delivery for private, listed, and public sector clients in the UAE (Dubai and Abu Dhabi), Saudi Arabia (Riyadh), and Korea (Seoul), including government agencies and sovereign led entities. The team came from the Middle East and Africa, the Americas, and Asia. I built the enterprise B2B pipeline for [allumni.ai](https://www.allumni.ai/), MBLM's generative AI platform, through presales and custom client demos, and I designed more than 25 AI agent and persona templates.

**Interim CEO, [Madison Technologies](https://madison-technologies.com/), 2024, Singapore.** I led the company through organizational change. Teams and colocation offices were in Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Manila, and Da Nang, serving enterprise and conglomerate clients and Malaysian government agencies. I made Madison a [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/) partner and a [ClickUp](https://clickup.com/) partner by setting the strategy and running the staff training required for partner eligibility.

**Cofounder and CEO, Kempus, Aug 2022 to Jan 2024, Singapore.** I cofounded a verified student community platform, raised $3 million in seed funding, and grew it to 33 universities across the United States. Teams sat in a Singapore HQ, Ho Chi Minh City, Da Nang, and the US. I owned product, engineering, fundraising, and hiring, managed partnerships in the US, Korea, and Israel, and introduced RAG based career guidance for students.

### Global engineering, 2017 to 2022

**Vice President of Engineering, [Quincus](https://quincus.com/), Jan 2021 to Aug 2022, Singapore.** I led the engineering department, more than 70 developers across 8 countries, in colocation and remote setups. We delivered supply chain visibility and logistics products for key clients in the US and for clients in China and Southeast Asia (Singapore, Thailand, and Malaysia). I led architecture for high availability, high throughput API and integration platforms that absorb burst traffic from enterprise customers, carriers, and partners, including work supporting [UPS](https://www.ups.com/). That covered API gateways, middleware, ERP, WMS, OMS, and CRM. I also led applied AI and machine learning for dynamic rating, logistics forecasting, routing, and market intelligence. I set up tech hubs in Sofia, Kyiv, Ho Chi Minh City, and Da Nang.

**Chief Technology Officer, [WorldRoamer](https://www.worldroamer.com/), Dec 2018 to Dec 2020, Singapore.** I hired and led the full technology team, expat and local, from the ground up at the Singapore HQ, and I managed offshore development agencies in Bulgaria (Sofia) and Vietnam. I owned technology strategy, architecture, and security for an online travel platform with channel partners in the US, Japan, and Southeast Asia. We integrated CRS and channel managers for more than 50,000 listings at MVP and added ML fraud detection.

**Vice President of Engineering, [RingMD](https://www.ring.md/), Jul 2017 to Sep 2018, Singapore.** I led the Singapore engineering team for a telemedicine platform serving healthcare organizations and government clients in Southeast Asia (Singapore and the Philippines), India, and the US. I supported the [Singapore Ministry of Health](https://www.moh.gov.sg/) telemedicine regulatory sandbox and expansion with the [Federal Government of India](https://www.india.gov.in/) through the Common Services Centre network. We delivered video consultation, [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/) CRM, chatbot, and wearable IoT features on sensitive health data.

### Creative technology in Seoul, 2009 to 2017

**Director, Head of Creative Technology Lab, GROUP IDD, Jun 2016 to Jun 2017, Seoul.** I led the lab that built digital products and campaign technology for [Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/), [Hyundai](https://www.hyundai.com/worldwide/en), [Kia](https://worldwide.kia.com/en), [L'Oréal](https://www.loreal.com/en/), [3M](https://www.3m.com/), [Burberry](https://www.burberry.com/), and the [Korea Tourism Organization](https://knto.or.kr/).

The agency case study reported more than 90% share of popular posts for the target hashtag and more than 15% growth in hashtag activity.

For 3M Post-it in 2017, I led creative technology for a Facebook Messenger chatbot and a Back to School campaign, linking social content, chatbot participation, product discovery, and rewards.
